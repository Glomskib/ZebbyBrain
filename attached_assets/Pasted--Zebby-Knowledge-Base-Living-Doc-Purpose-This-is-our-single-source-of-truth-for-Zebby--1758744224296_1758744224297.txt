## 1) One-Page Executive Summary

**Zebby is a** health tracker + social app + marketplace
**For** people with EDS (Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome) and other rare/odd health conditions who feel misunderstood by others, especially doctors
**That helps them** track symptoms, feel understood, connect with others, and access discounted EDS-related products
**By** offering AI-assisted symptom journaling, auto-tagging, community support, and a curated product marketplace
**Unlike** generic health trackers or forums, **Zebby** combines tracking, validation, social belonging, and access to relevant products in one integrated platform.

**North Star Metric (NSM):** % of users who log symptoms ≥3 times/week and report “feeling understood” (measured via in-app pulse surveys + avatar feedback).
**NSM Guardrails:** retention %, marketplace conversion rate, latency of AI insights, user privacy satisfaction.

**Launch target:** Webapp MVP (store + tracker), before Kickstarter/Shark Tank campaign.
**Stage:** Pre-MVP (concept shaping).

## 2) Problem → JTBD → Solution

### 2.1 Problem Statements

* People with EDS and similar conditions feel dismissed by medical professionals and lack tools that make sense of their daily struggles.
* Existing trackers are too clinical or generic, failing to capture the lived experience and emotional context.
* Accessing affordable, relevant products is fragmented and costly.

### 2.2 Jobs-To-Be-Done (JTBD)

* **When I** experience unexplained or fluctuating symptoms
  **I want to** log them in a way that feels validating and insightful
  **So I can** better understand my body, share my journey, and feel less alone.

* **When I** shop for EDS-related or supportive health products
  **I want to** access vetted, discounted options
  **So I can** save money and trust what I’m buying.

### 2.3 Solution Overview

* **System boundary:** Zebby includes health tracking, AI-assisted insights, community/social features, and marketplace access. It intentionally excludes clinical diagnosis or replacing professional care.
* **Value loops:**

  * Logging symptoms → receiving AI avatar reflection + stamped insights → building long-term health portrait → feeling validated → more logging.
  * Marketplace purchases → discounts → brand trust → repeat usage.
* **Critical path:** Signup → log first symptom → see AI avatar reflection + feel understood → browse store.

## 4) Competitive & Alternatives Map

* **Direct competitors:** Apple Health, MySymptoms, Bearable, PatientsLikeMe.
* **Indirect alternatives:** notebooks, spreadsheets, online forums, Amazon.
* **Positioning:** Zebby is where *tracking meets empathy*—not just numbers, but meaning.
* **Table stakes:** symptom logging, charts, data export, account privacy.
* **Differentiators:** AI avatar (core from MVP), social support baked in, curated discounted marketplace.

## 5) Product Scope & Features

### 5.1 MVP (v1)

* As a **user with EDS**, when I **log symptoms**, I can **see AI avatar reflections + stamped insights** so that **I feel validated and closer to understanding my condition**. (✅)
* As a **user with EDS**, when I **browse products**, I can **see categorized placeholders (tools, braces, supplements, salts/electrolytes, courses, etc.)** so that **I can save money and trust what I buy**. (✅)

**Non-goals:**

* Sleep tracking in v1 (mobile-only feature, deferred).
* Clinical/diagnostic features (out of scope).

## 7) Content & Brand System

* **Brand promise:** “You are understood.”
* **Voice & tone:** Empathetic, validating, hopeful.

  * Do: “That sounds tough. Let’s track it so we can understand together.”
  * Don’t: “Your symptoms don’t match medical criteria.”
* **Naming conventions:** Simple, human, non-clinical.
* **Accessibility:** Respect cognitive load; gentle color palette; readable type.

---

## 8) Data Model (Draft)

* Core object: **Symptom Log** (id, user\_id, symptom tags, notes, timestamp, AI avatar reflection).
* Other entities: User, Product, Order, Community Post, Avatar Interaction.
* Retention: Users can export and self-store; Zebby minimizes storage.

---

## 9) System Architecture (Draft)

* **Client:** Webapp first; later iOS/Android/Desktop.
* **Backend:** To be finalized (likely Node/TypeScript + Postgres).
* **Integrations:** Google Fit, heart rate monitors (general integrations if possible), Shopify (store), Stripe (payments), Google auth.

---

## 10) Security, Privacy, Compliance

* Only export for users (data stored externally if chosen).
* Research whether GDPR/CCPA applies; build consent + deletion flows if required.
* Privacy-first: no medical diagnosis; user controls data.

## 15) Risks & Assumptions

* **Market:** Will enough EDS/rare condition users pay \$15/mo without a free tier?
* **Product:** Avatar must feel supportive + educational without crossing into medical advice.
* **Tech:** Integration fragility (Google Fit, monitors).
* **Execution:** Funding dependent on Kickstarter/Shark Tank success.
* **Mitigation:** Social media strategy to build brand trust and loyalty.
