Zebby’s World – Feature & Function Checklist (from Living Doc)
1. MVP Scope (Q1 – Webapp first)

 Signup & Onboarding

Email/Google login (Stripe integration follows for trial → subscription).

Intro flow leading straight into first symptom log.

 Symptom Logging

Freeform journaling with text input.

Auto-tagging of symptom categories.

Timestamping, notes, and tags stored in structured “Symptom Log” object.

 AI Avatar Reflections

Avatar receives symptom log.

Returns empathetic reflection + “stamp” (visual marker).

Optional “feeling understood?” quick check-in for metrics.

 Marketplace (placeholder categories)

Categories visible: tools, braces, supplements, salts/electrolytes, courses.

Products initially placeholders (real vendor integration later).

Shopify + Stripe integration for checkout.

Discount framing visible.

 Critical Flow

Signup → log first symptom → see avatar reflection → browse store.

2. Post-MVP / Phase 2

 Community / Social Feed (P1)

Users can post, react, and connect (support/belonging).

Safety guardrails + moderation (to be defined).

 Data Export (P1)

Export symptom logs to:

Google Fit

CSV

PDF

 Mobile App Expansion (Q3)

Sleep tracking (deferred from MVP).

Deeper integrations (Google Fit, heart rate monitors).

 Community Expansion (Q4)

Avatar upgrades.

Enriched feed with social reinforcement.

3. Backend & Architecture

 Stack: Node/TypeScript + Postgres (draft).

 Integrations:

Shopify (storefront)

Stripe (payments + subscriptions)

Google Fit (exports)

OAuth (Google login)

 Data Model:

Symptom Log (core object).

User.

Product.

Order.

Community Post.

Avatar Interaction.

4. Analytics & Metrics

 Dashboards (first 5):

Acquisition (traffic → signup → activation).

Activation (% logging ≥3x/week).

Engagement (% reporting “feeling understood”).

Marketplace conversion % + ARPU.

Quality (AI latency + error rate).

 NSM: % of users logging ≥3x/week and reporting “feeling understood.”

 Guardrails: Retention %, marketplace conversion, latency, privacy satisfaction.

5. Security, Privacy, Compliance

 GDPR/CCPA consent + deletion flows.

 No HIPAA scope (not a diagnostic tool).

 Export-first philosophy: users control their data.

 Encryption at rest + in transit.

6. Monetization

 Trial-only model (no free tier).

 Subscription: $15/month.

 Trial length TBD (7 / 14 / 30 days — open question).

 Marketplace revenue: Shopify + Stripe.

7. Content & Brand

 Voice: empathetic, validating, hopeful.

 Branding: “You are understood.”

 UX: gentle palette, cognitive load–friendly.

 Avatar copywriting reflects support, not diagnosis.

8. Roadmap Anchors

Q1 → Webapp MVP (Tracker + Store + Avatar).

Q2 → Kickstarter / Shark Tank campaign.

Q3 → Mobile app (sleep + integrations).

Q4 → Community feed + avatar upgrades.

9. Open Questions (Needs Confirmation)

 Placeholder products vs. waiting for vendor partners.
